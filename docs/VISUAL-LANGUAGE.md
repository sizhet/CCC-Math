a structural center of gravity

recursive nucleus,
policy core,

“structural field” feeling

rather than flat diagrams.

Prefer:

radial layers,
differential layers,
recursive layers,
nested structures,
graph topologies.

flat flowcharts,

Figures should feel:

dense but calm

clutter,
excessive labels,
crowded arrows,
low-level implementation details.

The figure should communicate:

structural meaning first

not engineering noise.
